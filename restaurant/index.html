    Questions

    Things people ask us

    Not here? Ask on WhatsApp. We answer during working hours.

    Do you deliver to my home?

    Yes. We deliver in Mira Road and up to 3 km around. Send your order on WhatsApp with your address and we tell you the time.

    What is the minimum order?

    ₹300 for home delivery. There is no minimum for pickup or for eating here.

    Do you take party and bulk orders?

    Yes, for birthdays, office lunches and functions at home. Tell us the number of people one day before and we send a menu with the price.

    Can you make Jain food?

    Yes, on request. Most veg dishes can be made without onion, garlic and root vegetables. Write the word Jain in your WhatsApp message.

    Is veg food cooked separately?

    Yes. Veg food has its own section in the kitchen, with separate vessels and oil.

    How do I know you got my order?

    We reply on WhatsApp with the total and the time. Your order is confirmed only after that reply.
