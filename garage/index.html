                Questions

                Things people ask us

                Not here? Ask on WhatsApp and we will answer the same day.

                Will the final bill be more than the estimate?

                Only if we find something else that needs fixing. We send you a photo and the price on WhatsApp first, and we do that work only after you say yes.

                Do you use genuine parts?

                Yes. We use company parts or trusted brands, always with a bill. Your old parts go back in the boot so you can see what was changed.

                How far do you pick up and drop?

                Free within 5 km of the garage. Further than that, we charge a small fee that we tell you before we come.

                Will a service here affect my company warranty?

                No. We follow the service schedule in your owner's manual, use the right oil grade and stamp your service book.

                Is there a warranty on your work?

                Yes. Every service has a warranty of 1,000 km or 30 days, whichever comes first. If the same problem comes back, we fix it free.

                How can I pay?

                UPI, card or cash, after the work is done and you have checked the vehicle. You get a proper GST bill.
