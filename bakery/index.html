            Questions

            Things people ask us

            Not here? Ask on WhatsApp and we will answer the same day.

            Can every cake be made eggless?

            Most of them, yes. Cakes marked Eggless on the menu can be made without egg for ₹100 more a kilo. Our cupcakes and brownies are eggless at the same price.

            How early should I order?

            Order by 6 pm for a cake the next day. Theme cakes, tiers and photo cakes need two to three days.

            How do I pay?

            Pay 50% by UPI when you order, and the rest by UPI, card or cash when you get the cake.

            Do you make photo cakes?

            Yes. Send the photo on WhatsApp. We print it on an edible sheet and place it on the cake of your choice.

            When will my cake be delivered?

            You pick a two-hour slot when you order, between 10 am and 9 pm. We message you on WhatsApp when the cake leaves the shop.

            Do you have sugar-free cakes?

            Yes, with one day's notice. We make the chocolate and the fresh fruit cakes with a sugar-free sweetener. Ask for the price on WhatsApp.
