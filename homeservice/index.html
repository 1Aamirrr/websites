          Questions

          Things people ask us

          Not here? Ask on WhatsApp and we will answer within the hour.

          Can you really come today?

          Yes, for areas marked "same day", if a slot is free. Book at least 90 minutes before the slot starts and we confirm on WhatsApp within 10 minutes.

          Will the price change at my home?

          No. The service price is fixed. If your AC needs gas or a part, the technician shows you the problem and the price first. Nothing is done without your yes.

          Are the pest control chemicals safe for children and pets?

          We use government-approved, odourless gels and sprays. For most treatments you can stay at home. For bed bugs, we ask you to step out for 4 hours.

          What do I need to do before a deep cleaning?

          Just keep water and power on, and put away jewellery and papers. The team brings its own machines, ladders and cleaning material.

          What if the problem comes back?

          Every job has a 30-day warranty. Message us on WhatsApp and we come back and fix it, free.

          How do I pay?

          After the work is done and you have checked it. UPI, card or cash. You get a GST bill on WhatsApp.
