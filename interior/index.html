            Questions

            Things people ask us

            Not here? Ask on WhatsApp and we will answer the same day.

            Is the site visit really free?

            Yes. A designer comes to your home, measures every room and talks through what you want. You pay nothing and you are under no pressure to book.

            Do I see the design before I pay?

            Yes. You get a layout and 3D views of the main rooms first. You pay the 10% booking amount only when you are happy with the design.

            How long does a 2 BHK take?

            About 45 days from design sign-off for most 2 BHK homes. The furniture is made in our factory while the site work goes on, which saves weeks.

            What does the warranty cover?

            All modular woodwork, like the kitchen, wardrobes and TV unit, has a 10-year warranty. Hinges, channels and fittings carry the maker's own warranty.

            Can you work in an under-construction flat?

            Yes. We design early and start site work the day you get possession, so you can move in sooner.

            How do I pay?

            In four parts by UPI or bank transfer: 10% at booking, 50% when production starts, 35% at installation and 5% at handover. Every payment gets a GST bill.
