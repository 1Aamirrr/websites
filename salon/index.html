      Questions

      Things people ask us

      Not here? Ask on WhatsApp and we will answer the same day.

      Do I need an appointment?

      You can walk in, but evenings and weekends get busy. Book on WhatsApp and we keep your time ready.

      Which products do you use?

      Only sealed, branded salon products. Ask us and we will show you the pack before we use it.

      Do you come home for brides?

      Yes. Bridal make-up, hair and mehndi can be done at your home or the wedding hall anywhere in Mumbai. Travel is charged extra and we tell you the amount before you book.

      What if I need to cancel or change my time?

      Message us at least 3 hours before. There is no charge for changing a normal appointment. Bridal bookings need an advance, which we explain when you book.

      How can I pay?

      Cash, UPI and cards are all accepted at the counter. You pay after the service.

      Is there a separate area for men and women?

      Yes. The men's chairs are on their own side, and skin services are done in closed rooms.
