              Questions

              Things people ask us

              Not here? Ask on WhatsApp and we will answer the same day.

              Is there a joining fee?

              No. You pay only the price of the plan. There is no joining fee and no hidden charge.

              Can I pause my membership?

              Yes. You can pause a 3-month plan once for up to 15 days, and a 12-month plan for up to 30 days. Tell us on WhatsApp before you go.

              How does the free trial work?

              Book a time on WhatsApp and come in workout clothes. A trainer shows you the gym and takes you through one full session. You pay nothing.

              Is there a women-only batch?

              Yes. The 11 am batch from Monday to Friday is only for women, with a woman trainer on the floor.

              I have never been to a gym. Can I join?

              Yes. Most members start as beginners. Your trainer plans the first month with you and checks your form on every exercise.

              What should I bring?

              Clean sports shoes that you keep for the gym, a small towel and a water bottle. Lockers are free to use while you train.
