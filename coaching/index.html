              Questions parents ask

              Before you choose a class

              Not here? Ask on WhatsApp and we will answer the same day.

              Can we pay the fees in parts?

              Yes. You can pay in 3 parts: at admission, by 15 October and by 15 January. Paying the full year at admission costs a little less. UPI, card and cheque are all fine, and you get a receipt every time.

              How many students are in one batch?

              Never more than 20. Each teacher knows every student by name and can check their notebook in class.

              How will I know how my child is doing?

              Every Sunday there is a short test. Marks come to you on WhatsApp the same week, and a full report with the teacher's note comes every month. There is a parents' meeting every term.

              What if my child misses a class?

              They can sit the same topic in another batch that week, and the notes are shared on WhatsApp. For long leave, the teacher plans extra time for doubts.

              Do you teach SSC, CBSE and ICSE?

              Yes, for Std 8 to 10. The topics are matched to your child's board and school textbook. Std 11 and 12 follow the HSC and CBSE syllabus.

              Is the demo class really free?

              Yes. Your child joins one real class with the batch. There is no fee and no form to fill. Book it on WhatsApp and come 10 minutes early.
