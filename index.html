Questions

Common questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

Who owns the domain?

You do. The domain (for example yourbusiness.in) is bought in your name and you pay for it directly, so it is always yours.

Do I need to know computers?

No. You send photos and text on WhatsApp; we do the rest.

Can I change things later?

Yes. With monthly care, up to 5 small changes a month are included. Without it, we quote small changes separately.

How do customers contact me?

Every page has a WhatsApp button and a call button, so a customer reaches you in one tap. Forms send the message to your WhatsApp; nothing is stored on a server.

Will it show on Google?

It is built so it shows when people search your business name. Ranking for general searches takes time and is not promised.

How do I pay?

50% advance and 50% when the site is live, by UPI.
