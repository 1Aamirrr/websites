Your first visit

What happens when you come in

No referral needed. Bring any X-ray, MRI or doctor's note you have.

  1. Message or call

    Tell us what hurts and when you are free. We reply within the hour while the clinic is open.

  2. A full check

    Your therapist asks about the pain, checks how you move, and goes through your reports with you.

  3. A clear plan and cost

    You hear what is likely causing the pain, how many sessions you may need and the total cost, before you pay for a pack.

  4. Treatment and home exercises

    Treatment starts the same day. Your exercises come on WhatsApp with photos, so you can do them at home.

          Questions

          Things patients ask us

          Not here? Ask on WhatsApp and a therapist will answer the same day.

          Do I need a doctor's referral?

          No. You can book directly. If you have seen a doctor or have an X-ray or MRI, please bring it, so your therapist can see it.

          How many sessions will I need?

          Most people come for 6 to 10 sessions. Your therapist tells you after the first check, and you buy a pack only if you want one.

          What should I wear?

          Loose, comfortable clothes. Shorts help for knee and ankle problems, and a loose T-shirt for neck and shoulder. There is a changing room at the clinic.

          What does a home visit cost?

          A single home visit is ₹1,200, and packs cost less per session. There is no travel charge in Powai and the nearest areas. Farther areas have a small travel charge, shown in the timings section.

          Can I claim it on health insurance?

          We give a proper receipt with the therapist's registration number. Many health and company plans cover physiotherapy after surgery or with a doctor's note. Please check with your insurer.

          Will the treatment hurt?

          You may feel a little sore after the first few sessions, like after a workout. Your therapist works within what you can bear and changes the plan if something does not feel right.
