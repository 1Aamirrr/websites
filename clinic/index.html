    Questions

    Things patients ask us

    Not here? Ask on WhatsApp and we will answer during clinic hours.

    Does it hurt?

    For most treatments the area is numbed first with a local anaesthetic. You may still feel pressure, and some soreness afterwards is common. If you are nervous, tell us. We go slowly and stop when you raise your hand.

    How much does a check-up cost?

    A check-up starts from ₹500. If an X-ray or a cleaning is needed, we tell you the cost before we do it.

    Do you treat children?

    Yes. Children are welcome from their first tooth. First visits are kept short, and a parent stays with the child the whole time.

    What about emergencies?

    For strong pain, swelling, bleeding that does not stop or a knocked-out tooth, call the clinic. Please do not wait for a WhatsApp reply. If the clinic is closed, go to the nearest hospital.

    Call the clinic

    How can I pay?

    By cash, UPI or card. For treatments that take many visits, you can pay visit by visit.

    Can I change my appointment?

    Yes. Message or call us a few hours before your time, so we can give the slot to someone else.
